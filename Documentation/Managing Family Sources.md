# Managing Family Sources

Bim.FamilyManager allows you to connect to multiple sources for your Revit family libraries. These sources can be local or network directories, or Azure Storage containers. Managing your sources enables you to organize, browse, and synchronize families from different locations within the Revit environment.

---

## How to Add a New Family Source

1. **Open the Bim.FamilyManager Add-in**
   - Launch Revit and select the "scotec" tab.
   - Click on "Bim.FamilyManager" to open the main interface.

2. **Access Settings**
   - In the Bim.FamilyManager window, click the "Settings" or "Preferences" button (typically a gear icon).
   - Navigate to the "Family Sources" section.

3. **Add a New Family Source**
   - Click the "Add Source" button.
   - Choose the type of source: [Directory](Adding%20a%20Directory%20Family%20Source.md) or [Azure Storage](Adding%20an%20Azure%20Family%20Source.md).
   - Follow the instructions in the relevant section to complete the setup.
