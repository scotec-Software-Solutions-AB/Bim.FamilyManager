Calculate tints:
r = color.R + (255 - color.R) * tintFactor);
g = color.G + (255 - color.G) * tintFactor);
b = color.B + (255 - color.B) * tintFactor);

Used tint factors: 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8

#99 > 0.2 = AD

#00 > 0.4 = 66
